Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 25, 2026

Wayfare stores your game progress and preferences locally on your device. If you use export or import, that backup remains under your control and is processed on your device.

The free version uses Google AdMob to show advertising. AdMob may process information such as device identifiers, IP address, advertising interactions, diagnostics, and approximate location to deliver, measure, prevent fraud, and improve ads. Where required, Wayfare asks for consent before requesting ads, requests iOS tracking permission before accessing the advertising identifier, and provides ad privacy choices in Settings. Wayfare Plus subscribers do not receive ad requests.

Purchases are processed by Apple and subscription status is managed through RevenueCat. Wayfare does not receive your full payment details. RevenueCat may process app user identifiers, purchase history, and diagnostics needed to provide and restore purchases.

These services operate under their own policies: Google Privacy Policy and RevenueCat Privacy Policy.

Retention and deletion

Local saves and preferences remain on your device until you replace them, clear the app's data, or delete Wayfare. Wayfare does not operate an account database or server containing your game save. Apple, Google, and RevenueCat retain information according to their own policies and legal obligations.

You can review or change eligible advertising consent choices from Wayfare's Settings screen. Deleting Wayfare removes its locally stored game data. For help with privacy requests, inappropriate ads, or purchase access, visit Wayfare Support.

We do not sell personal information.
