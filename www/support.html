Wayfare Support

Need help with your life, a purchase, or an ad? Use the options below.

Purchases

Open Wayfare, tap the ✦ button, then choose Restore Purchases. Monthly subscriptions can be managed from your Apple ID subscription settings.

Game progress

Wayfare saves progress on your device. In Settings, use Copy backup before changing devices or deleting the app, then use Restore to import it.

Report a problem or inappropriate ad

Open a support report. Include your Wayfare version, device model, iOS version, and what happened. Do not include payment details, passwords, or other sensitive information.
