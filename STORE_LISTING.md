## Description (4000 char max)

Every life starts the same way: a name, a birth year, and one button.

Wayfare is a full life simulator that plays out one year at a time. Age up,
and watch your choices compound — school, career, relationships, money,
health, and the family you build along the way. There's no script. There's
no reset button that undoes a bad decade. There's just the next year, and
what you do with it.

WHAT YOU CAN DO
• Build a career from your first job to running multiple businesses
• Date, marry, have kids, and raise them into their own lives
• Buy homes and cars, finance them, or default and start over
• Adopt and name companions, each with a personality and a bond you earn
• Travel the world one trip a year — 16 destinations, one passport
• Write a bucket list at 18 and spend a lifetime crossing it off
• Chase 35 achievements, from surviving to 100 to founding a dynasty
• Play the market — stocks, bonds, crypto — and live with the outcome
• Die, and — if you've built something worth inheriting — play on as
  your own child in a new generation

NO TWO LIVES ARE THE SAME
Every playthrough branches on hundreds of small choices. A safe adulthood
into a company job looks nothing like betting everything on a startup at
22. Both are valid. Both have consequences. Wayfare doesn't tell you which
one is right.

BUILT FOR QUICK SESSIONS OR LONG ONES
Age up in seconds between meetings, or sit down and play out a whole
lifetime start to finish. Your progress and achievements carry across
every life you play.

Free to play. Wayfare Plus (optional) removes ads and unlocks the Time
Machine — one chance per life to undo your own death — plus exclusive
pets, including a tiny dragon who is technically a very committed iguana.

## Notes for App Review (Version 2.0)
Wayfare does not require an account or demo login.

This update adds Crossroads: eight authored three-part stories, six yearly intentions, annual summaries, and an on-device Chronicle of choices and inherited family memories. All core progression is free. No generative AI or account is required to play.

Crossroads stories begin from age 6. Tap the intention card on Life to choose a focus before aging up. Open More > Your Chronicle to view decisions, yearly summaries, and family records. The Life screen's "The rest of your world" section contains existing goals and the bucket list.

Purchase fixes register the official RevenueCat Capacitor SDK, grant Plus only from the active `plus` entitlement, handle pending approvals without repeated checkouts, refresh verified access, and restore existing purchases. Tap the top-right Plus button for monthly, lifetime, Restore Purchases, and Check purchase status.

The following existing features are retained:
- Travel (More > Travel): from age 18, the player can take one simulated trip per in-game year to one of 16 real-world destinations, paid with in-game money. Trips are text and illustration only; there is no booking, location access, network call, or real-world purchase involved.
- A Bucket List on the Life tab from age 18: five in-game goals that complete automatically. Stored only in the local save.
- A new life ambition and five achievements. All progression is free; nothing in this update is gated behind Wayfare Plus.

Earlier updates added:
- Companions: adopted pets are named by the player (free text, on-device only), have a personality trait, and a bond that grows through yearly care actions and decays through neglect. Everything is stored in the local save; no account, network, or user-generated-content sharing is involved. Reach it via the People tab.
- A "What's new" sheet shown once per release to returning players, and again on demand from Settings.
- New multi-year life dilemmas and visible choice stakes.
- A refreshed Life screen and decision interface in light and dark themes.
- Daily Journeys rotate by local calendar day and persist only on-device. Players complete age-ups, choices, Activities, or relationship actions to build a streak and claim modest in-game rewards. No account, notification permission, or network connection is required.
- An optional review prompt after age 18 and at least 12 age-ups in one session. The Wayfarer’s Compass reward is granted to every eligible player before either prompt option is selected; it is not conditional on leaving a review.
- The Wayfarer’s Compass adds a third yearly Focus action and appears under More > Activities.
- Interactive 3D home rooms: tap More > Home, choose Browse Properties,
  rent any property with Move In Empty, then tap the room/floor and drag to
  move and look around.
- Wayfare Free Drive: tap More > Vehicles after purchasing a street car,
  then tap Free Drive. Controls, camera switching, traffic, ramps, speed,
  RPM, and vehicle-specific performance are available in the driving view.
- Advertising for free players: interstitial ads appear only at natural
  breaks after five completed age-ups, beginning when the simulated
  character is age 8. Ads never appear for Wayfare Plus customers.
- Wayfare Plus: tap the ✦ button in the top-right corner. The monthly
  auto-renewable subscription and lifetime non-consumable both grant the
  same "plus" entitlement: no ads, one Time Machine undo per life,
  exclusive pets, and a Plus legacy badge. Restore Purchases is on the same
  screen.
